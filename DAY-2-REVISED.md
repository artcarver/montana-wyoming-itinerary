## Smoke & Air Quality — Sunday, August 9, 2026

**Bottom line: today is a genuinely smoky day, worst in the morning, clearing as the day heats up. Flip the day so the hard hiking happens in the afternoon, not at 9 AM.**

### Conditions as of this morning
| Location | Reading | Category |
|---|---|---|
| West Glacier (6:00 AM) | AQI 160 · PM2.5 68.7 µg/m³ | **Unhealthy** |
| Kalispell (7:00 AM) | AQI 224 · PM2.5 149 µg/m³ | **Very Unhealthy** |
| Columbia Falls (yesterday 7 AM) | Unhealthy | — |

- **Active DEQ air quality alert** covering **Flathead County** (plus Lincoln, Lake, Sanders, Missoula, Ravalli and ~12 more), in effect **until 9:00 AM today**. Expect it to be extended or reissued.
- **Nearest driver:** a wildfire near **Trego, MT** (~50 mi west of Hungry Horse) is the local contributor. The larger regional load is smoke from **large fires in Washington and Oregon** plus the Montana fires in the Pioneer/Sapphire ranges (Bobcat Lakes ~4,300 ac, Sand Creek ~5,300 ac, Moose Fire ~2,300 ac).
- **No active fire inside Glacier NP** and no fire-related closures on the west side as of this morning — this is transported smoke, not a park emergency. Avalanche Creek, Trail of the Cedars, and the Apgar area are all open.

### Today's trend — the important part
Overnight **inversions trap smoke in the valleys** (Hungry Horse and the Lake McDonald valley hold it especially well), and it **mixes out as the ground heats up mid-morning**. The hourly forecast shows the improvement clearly:

- **6–9 AM:** worst of it — AQI ~160 at West Glacier, ~224 in Kalispell
- **Mid-morning:** inversion breaks, atmosphere starts mixing
- **Afternoon:** improving — West Glacier easing toward ~136–138
- **Evening/overnight:** continued improvement, Kalispell down toward ~90 by midnight as winds pick up

**NWS West Glacier forecast today:** "Smoke. Sunny, high near 83. Southwest wind 1–5 mph." Tonight: "Areas of smoke. Mostly clear, low around 55."

### What this means for you
- **The 8:30 AM start is an advantage today, not a cost.** You are skipping the dirtiest two hours of air.
- Avalanche Lake is 5.8 miles round trip with sustained effort — do it **after** the air improves, not first thing.
- Do the **flat, short, shady** things while the air is still bad. Trail of the Cedars is 1 flat mile under dense old-growth canopy — low exertion, minimal deep breathing.
- **Carry N95s** if you have them; a cloth buff does nothing for PM2.5. If either of you gets a headache, scratchy throat, or chest tightness, that's the cue to cut the hike short — the lake at the 2-mile mark is not worth a bad night before Going-to-the-Sun Road.
- **Views will be hazy.** Ridgelines beyond ~3–5 miles will wash out. Avalanche Lake's cliff walls are close enough to still read well; distant Lake McDonald panoramas will not. Adjust photo expectations, favor close subjects (cedars, the gorge, the lakeshore rocks).
- **Recheck before you leave:** [fire.airnow.gov](https://fire.airnow.gov) is the best single source — live monitors plus the smoke plume layer.

## Revised Schedule

The core reordering: **drive straight to Avalanche Creek to secure parking, save Lake McDonald Lodge for the way back, and drop Johns Lake Loop.** With an 8:30 departure you cannot fit a 2-mile warm-up loop and still park at Avalanche.

- **8:15 AM — Breakfast: eat in the car** _[Food]_
  ✅ Park pass already in hand — use the pass-holder lane at the booth.
  Use the breakfast groceries from the Bozeman stop and eat on the 15-minute drive. **See the breakfast options section below** if you want something hot — the short version is that a sit-down breakfast and Avalanche Creek parking are mutually exclusive this morning.

- **8:30 AM — Depart Mini Golden Inns** _[Travel]_  _(▸ 15 min to West Glacier)_
  Hungry Horse → West Glacier entrance on Hwy 2.
  Bring more water than you think — 83°F and smoke both dry you out. Fill bottles at the hotel.

- **8:45 AM — West Glacier Entrance** _[Viewpoint]_
  Straight through, no timed-entry reservation required in 2026.
  Don't stop at the sign for photos yet — parking is the clock you're racing. Photos on the way out.

- **9:15 AM — Avalanche Creek Parking** _[Logistics]_  _(▸ ~30 min from entrance)_
  ⚠️ **This is the tight part of the day.** The lot historically fills around 9:30 AM and stays full into late afternoon.
  If the lot is full: **do not circle.** Continue up Going-to-the-Sun to a pullout, or drop back to Lake McDonald Lodge, do the lodge and shoreline stop now, and retry Avalanche around 3:30–4:00 PM when the morning crowd clears out. Late afternoon is also the cleanest air of the day, so the fallback is a genuinely good outcome, not a consolation prize.

- **9:30 AM — Trail of the Cedars** _[Trail]_  _(▲ 1 mi loop / flat)_
  Flat boardwalk through ancient red cedar and hemlock. **Deliberately placed first — lowest exertion during the smokiest hour.**
  Towering old-growth, ferns, and the gorge where Avalanche Creek carves turquoise water through red rock. The canopy is dense enough that this feels sheltered. Take it slow, this is the stop that rewards slowness anyway. ~45 min.

- **10:30 AM — Avalanche Lake** _[Trail]_  _(▲ 5.8 mi RT / 500 ft gradual)_
  The main event, now starting as the inversion breaks and air begins improving.
  The trailhead branches off the Trail of the Cedars boardwalk. 500 ft of gain spread over nearly 3 miles — it never feels steep. Budget **3 to 3.5 hours** round trip including a real sit at the lake. The cliff headwall with its waterfall streaks is close-range scenery, so smoke haze hurts it far less than it hurts distant views.
  **Turnaround rule:** if either of you is feeling the air by the halfway point, turn around. The gorge and cedars already made the morning worth it.

- **2:00 PM — Late lunch at the lake or trailhead** _[Food]_
  Whatever you packed — eat at the lake if you're still up there, or at the car.

- **2:45 PM — Lake McDonald Lodge** _[Viewpoint]_  _(▸ ~20 min back down)_
  Moved from morning to afternoon. Historic Swiss chalet, massive stone fireplace, right on the lake.
  Walk the dark-wood lobby, then down to the shore where red, green, and blue rocks show through the clear water. Cold drinks inside, and it's an easy indoor break from the smoke. 30–40 min.

- **3:45 PM — Apgar Village** _[Viewpoint]_
  Visitor center, lakeshore, gift shop, and the ice cream you earned.
  Browse the displays and walk the Apgar shore. Glacier Park Boat Co. runs ~1.5 hr Lake McDonald cruises from the dock — **if you want it, ask at the dock when you arrive**, and note that boat time on a smoky day is mostly hazy distance views. The famous colorful rocks along the shoreline are the better bet today: they're at your feet, and smoke can't touch them.

- **5:00 PM — Back to the hotel / decompress** _[Lodging]_
  15 min back to Hungry Horse. Shower off the smoke, rehydrate, repack for tomorrow.
  Tomorrow is Going-to-the-Sun Road with an early start and **Logan Pass parking fills by 9 AM** — a real rest window here matters more than squeezing in one more stop.

- **8:00 PM — Lake McDonald Sunset** _[Viewpoint]_ — **optional**
  Last chance on the west side; tomorrow you cross to the east and don't come back.
  **Smoke silver lining:** heavy particulate turns sunsets deep red-orange, and the sun goes soft and disc-like well before the horizon. This may be the most dramatic sunset of the trip. Air is also at its cleanest of the day by now. Sunset 8:52 PM. If you caught it last night and you're tired, skipping this is the right call.

- **10:30 PM — Night Sky — Aurora Watch** _[Night Sky]_ — **likely skip tonight**
  Smoke is the enemy of dark-sky viewing — it scatters light and mutes everything, aurora included.
  Check SpaceWeatherLive; **Kp 5+ is the threshold worth losing sleep for at 48°N**, and tonight you'd need it to punch through haze on top of that. With "areas of smoke" in the overnight forecast, the realistic call is: step outside for five minutes, look north, then go to bed. Tomorrow is the biggest driving day of the Glacier stretch.

## Breakfast Options — In and Near the Park

The constraint: **a sit-down breakfast and a 9:15 AM arrival at Avalanche Creek cannot both happen.** Pick one.

| Place | Where | Hours | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eddie's Cafe & Gifts** | Apgar Village — ~2 min off route, just past the entrance | Opens **8:00 AM**, breakfast till 11:15 | Omelets, pancakes, **breakfast burritos**. Order at the counter to go — costs ~15 min total. |
| **Russell's Fireside Dining Room** | Lake McDonald Lodge — directly on route | **6:30–10:00 AM** | Full hot buffet + lighter continental option. First-come, **no reservations**. The best food, but 45–60 min seated. |
| **West Glacier Restaurant & Bar** | 200 Going-to-the-Sun Rd — outside the entrance | Opens **7:30 AM**, breakfast till 10:30 | Full menu. 406-888-5662. |
| **Carolyn's Cafe** | Hungry Horse — right by the hotel | Breakfast served late morning | At a gas station, locally well-regarded. Closest to the door. |

**Recommended:** eat the Bozeman groceries in the car, and if you want something hot, grab an **Eddie's breakfast burrito to go** in Apgar. Protecting the Avalanche parking window is worth more this morning than a table.

**The alternative, if you'd rather sit down:** take **Russell's** and consciously flip to the afternoon fallback — Lake McDonald Lodge and Apgar in the morning, Avalanche Creek at 3:30–4:00 PM. That's a legitimately good version of the day: cleanest air, emptied lot, and an 8:52 PM sunset gives you plenty of light for a 4:00 PM start. The trade-off is that it consumes the evening Lake McDonald sunset stop.

⚠️ Both in-park spots are concession-operated and hours shift late in the season — worth a quick confirm if you're routing the morning around one.
